Battleboat.js

A JavaScript AI that beats humans at battleship.

How to play

  1. Select your ships from the left-hand panel
  2. Place your ships on your map
  3. Click the enemy's grid to find and destroy all five ships
  4. The computer fires back immediately after your shot
